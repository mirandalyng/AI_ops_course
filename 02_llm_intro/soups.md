**1. Tom Yum Goong (Thai Hot & Sour Soup)**  
A fragrant, spicy-sour Thai classic brimming with shrimp, lemongrass, lime, and galangal. It’s celebrated for its bold, refreshing flavors and aromatic herb profile that awakens the palate.

- Fresh shrimp, peeled and deveined  
- Lemongrass stalk, bruised  
- Galangal root, sliced  
- Kaffir lime leaves, torn  
- Fish sauce and lime juice  
- Chili peppers, sliced  
- Mushrooms (straw or button)  
- Water or chicken stock  
- Cilantro and green onions for garnish  

- Bring water or stock to a boil, then add lemongrass, galangal, and kaffir lime leaves; simmer 5 minutes to extract aromatics.  
- Add shrimp and mushrooms, cook until shrimp turn pink and mushrooms soften.  
- Stir in fish sauce, lime juice, and sliced chilies; adjust seasoning to balance salty, sour, and heat.  
- Garnish with cilantro and green onions, serve hot with jasmine rice or as a starter.

**2. Miso Soup (Japanese Traditional Soup)**  
A staple of Japanese cuisine, this simple yet umami-rich soup features fermented miso paste, dashi stock, and optional add-ins like tofu and seaweed. It’s valued for its comforting warmth and gut-friendly probiotics.

- Dashi stock (kelp and bonito broth)  
- White miso paste  
- Firm tofu, cubed  
- Dried wakame seaweed, rehydrated  
- Green onions, finely chopped  

- Gently heat dashi in a pot; avoid boiling once miso is added to preserve flavor and nutrients.  
- Whisk miso paste with a small amount of warm broth in a separate bowl until smooth, then stir back into the pot.  
- Add tofu and wakame, heating just until warmed through (about 2–3 minutes).  
- Sprinkle with chopped green onions and serve immediately in small bowls.

**3. Phở Bò (Vietnamese Beef Noodle Soup)**  
A national treasure of Vietnam, pho features slow-simmered beef bones spiced with star anise, cinnamon, and cloves, served over rice noodles with thinly sliced raw beef that cooks in the hot broth. It’s beloved for its deep, complex broth and fresh herb accompaniments.

- Beef bones (marrow and knuckle) with oxtail, roasted  
- Charred onion and ginger for depth  
- Spices: star anise, cinnamon stick, cloves, cardamom  
- Fish sauce, rock sugar  
- Rice noodles (bánh phở)  
- Thinly sliced beef (eye round or brisket)  
- Bean sprouts, Thai basil, lime wedges, cilantro, and chili for serving  

- Roast beef bones and char onion/ginger on a dry pan or oven until blackened; transfer to a large pot with water and bring to a boil, then skim impurities.  
- Add spices, fish sauce, and rock sugar; reduce to a gentle simmer and cook uncovered 2–3 hours, occasionally skimming, until broth is rich and aromatic.  
- Strain broth, discard spices, and keep warm. Briefly blanch rice noodles, then divide among bowls. Top with raw beef slices, pour hot broth over to cook the beef, and arrange bean sprouts, herbs, lime, and chili on the side for customization.
