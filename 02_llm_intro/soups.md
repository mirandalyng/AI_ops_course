**1. Tom Yum Goong (Thai Spicy‑Sour Shrimp Soup)**  
A vibrant Thai staple celebrated for its hot‑and-sour punch, bright aromatics like lemongrass and kaffir lime, and tender shrimp simmered in a fragrant broth.  

- Fresh shrimp, peeled and deveined  
- Lemongrass, finely minced  
- Kaffir lime leaves, torn  
- Galangal, sliced  
- Fish sauce and fresh lime juice  
- Thai bird’s eye chili (or to taste)  
- Water or light chicken stock  
- sliced mushrooms and cherry tomatoes (optional)  

**Steps:**  
1. Bring water (or stock) to a boil, add lemongrass, galangal, and kaffir lime leaves; simmer 5 minutes to infuse.  
2. Add shrimp and mushrooms; cook just until shrimp turns pink and opaque.  
3. Stir in fish sauce, lime juice, and sliced chili; taste and adjust seasoning.  
4. Garnish with cilantro and serve hot with jasmine rice or as a standalone soup.

**2. Miso Soup (Japanese Dashi Soup)**  
A minimalist, umami‑rich staple that showcases the delicate balance of fermented miso and clear dashi, often complemented by silky tofu and ocean‑fresh nori.  

- Dashi stock (traditional kombu‑bonito base or high‑quality instant dashi)  
- White miso paste  
- Firm tofu, cut into small cubes  
- Dried nori strips  
- Green onions, thinly sliced  
- Dried wakame (optional, rehydrated)  

**Steps:**  
1. Heat dashi in a small pot over low heat; bring barely to a simmer—do not boil, as this can make the broth bitter.  
2. Dissolve miso paste in a ladle of warm dashi, then gently stir the mixture back into the pot.  
3. Add tofu cubes and nori; heat gently for 2 minutes, just to warm through.  
4. Top with sliced green onions and serve immediately in small ceramic bowls.

**3. Pho Bo (Vietnamese Beef Noodle Soup)**  
The iconic Vietnamese noodle soup built on a clear, aromatic beef broth simmered for hours with charred aromatics and warm spices, served over rice noodles and customized with fresh herbs, lime, and chili.  

- Beef bones (marrow and knuckle) for a rich, clear broth  
- Charred onion and ginger, halved  
- Whole spices: star anise, cinnamon stick, cloves, green cardamom  
- Fish sauce and a touch of rock sugar for balance  
- Flat rice noodles (bánh pho)  
- Thinly sliced raw beef brisket or eye round  
- Fresh garnishes: bean sprouts, Thai basil, cilantro, lime wedges, sliced bird’s eye chili  

**Steps:**  
1. Roast onion and ginger over an open flame or broiler until charred; place beef bones in a large pot, cover with water, and bring to a boil. Skim any foam, then reduce to a gentle simmer.  
2. Add charred aromatics and whole spices; simmer uncovered for at least 2–3 hours, occasionally adding a splash of water if needed. Strain the broth and discard solids.  
3. Season the broth with fish sauce and rock sugar; keep warm over low heat.  
4. Blanch rice noodles in hot water just until pliable (about 30 seconds); drain.  
5. Divide noodles into bowls, layer raw beef slices on top, and ladle hot broth over the beef—the residual heat will gently poach the meat.  
6. Garnish with bean sprouts, herbs, lime wedges, and chili; let everyone customize their bowl just before eating.
