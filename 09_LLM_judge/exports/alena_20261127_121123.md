Short answer: **no, you can't just vibe code your way into AI engineering.** AI coding tools are great helpers, but they're not a substitute for fundamentals.

You need to learn:
- **Python** (actually understand it, not just copy-paste)
- **Math** (linear algebra, calculus, probability & statistics)
- **Machine learning basics** (overfitting, evaluation metrics, how models learn)
- **Deep learning** (neural networks, transformers, attention)
- **Systems/MLOps** (GPUs, deployment, debugging production issues)

Use AI tools like Copilot or Cursor as pair programmers—let them write boilerplate and explain errors—but make sure you can explain every line of code yourself. When your model breaks at 2 a.m., you need to know why, not just hope the AI fixes it.

Start with fundamentals, build small projects from scratch, then specialize. The best AI engineers are the ones who understand the system well enough to know when the AI is wrong.
